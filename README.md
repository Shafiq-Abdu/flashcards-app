# 📘 AI Flashcard Generator — Quick Manual

🔗 **Live App**  
https://shafiq-abdu.github.io/flashcards-app/

---

## 🚀 What this app does

The AI Flashcard Generator converts **notes or PDFs into study flashcards** automatically using AI.

It is useful for:
- Exam preparation  
- Concept revision  
- Mathematical formulas (LaTeX supported)  
- Quick review sessions  

---

## 1️⃣ How to Create a Deck

### Option A — Upload a PDF
1. Click **Choose Files**
2. Upload a PDF (text-based works best)
3. Click **Generate deck**

✅ Tip: If you can select text in the PDF, it will work well.

---

### Option B — Paste Notes
1. Paste text into the notes box  
2. Click **Generate deck**

Good for:
- Lecture notes  
- Textbook summaries  
- Definitions & formulas  

---

## 2️⃣ Study Mode

Once cards are generated:

### Flip a Card
- Click/tap the card  
OR  
- Press the **Flip** button  

---

### Navigate Cards
- **Next / Previous** buttons  
- Shuffle for random order  

---

### Difficulty Marking
Cards can be labeled:
- Again  
- Hard  
- Good  
- Easy  

This helps track revision quality.

---

## 3️⃣ Saving & Loading

### Save Deck
Stores cards locally in your browser.

### Load Deck
Reopen saved decks later.

⚠️ Decks are stored locally (not cloud synced).

---

## ✅ Best Practices

- Use clear notes  
- One concept per paragraph  
- Generate 10–25 cards for quality  
- Text-based PDFs work best  

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Scanned PDFs may not work (OCR needed)  
- No cloud sync yet  
- AI output may need quick review
